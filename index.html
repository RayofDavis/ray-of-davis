About

An artist’s eye.
A human approach.

I’m Rachel Davis, the artist, photographer, and graphic designer behind Ray of Davis. I make portraits, campaign visuals, and original artwork for people with something to share.

Photographing athletes taught me to anticipate a moment. Designing sports campaigns taught me to turn that energy into a bigger story. I bring both to your project: an eye for the image and a plan for how it works.

From a personal commission to a brand launch, I connect photography, art, and design around one clear idea—so your work looks like you, not everyone else.
