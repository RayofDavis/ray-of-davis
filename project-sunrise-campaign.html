Behind the lens

Sunrise homecoming weekend

Homecoming is one weekend, and the light only lasts an hour. I planned around the sunrise and let the crowd arrive on its own.

I kept the frames close and unposed, so the morning reads as it felt: quiet, a little early, and full of people coming back to where they started.
